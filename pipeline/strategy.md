Cloudinary's stated direction, from its public announcements (2026):

- AI agents and agent-native development are the headline bet. Cloudinary launched AI agents for visual media teams (May 2026), all built on its MCP servers, then an "agent experience" layer (August 2026) with MCP support, Skills packages and AI-optimized SDKs, so that AI coding agents can build on Cloudinary directly.
- Developers stay central: the developer community passed four million members (July 2026), and SDKs are part of the agent push.
- AI-driven media features keep growing: image to video at scale (June 2026), AI video publishing in MediaFlows (April 2026), AI moderation for brand control (March 2026).
- Enterprise DAM and governance: named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for DAM; C2PA content credentials for AI-generated images (October 2026), tied to the EU AI Act.
